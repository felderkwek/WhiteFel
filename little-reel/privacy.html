How Little Reel handles the media you select, saved projects, exports, and information you send to support.

Privacy policy · Effective 23 November 2026 · Developer: WhiteFel

Selected photos and videos

Little Reel uses the photos and videos you choose to create previews, edits, and exported movies. Selected media is imported into the app’s storage so it can be edited. You choose which items to provide through Apple’s Photos picker.

If an item is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple may download it as part of your selection. That is handled by Apple and your device’s Photos settings.

Local projects and editing

Saved montage projects, editing settings, and imported project media are stored in Little Reel’s local app storage. The editing features process this media to create previews and MP4 exports. Little Reel does not provide a cloud-sync service or require a user account.

Device backups and system-level services, including any backup services you enable through Apple, are controlled by your device settings and Apple’s policies.

Advertising and purchases

Little Reel does not contain advertising. It is planned as a paid app with a one-time purchase. Purchases and downloads through the App Store are processed by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. WhiteFel does not receive your payment-card details.

Information you send to support

If you email WhiteFel, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as a description of a problem, device details, or attachments. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate issues. Email is handled through the email providers involved in the correspondence.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to respond to and resolve the request, maintain a record of the issue, and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence.

Sharing and exports

When you export a movie or use the system share sheet, you choose the destination. A service or app you choose to share with handles the exported file under its own terms and privacy practices.

Managing and deleting your data

You can delete saved montage projects from Little Reel’s gallery. This removes the app’s project copy and does not delete the original media in Photos. Removing the app removes its local app storage, subject to your device’s backup and restore settings. Movies already saved or shared elsewhere must be managed at those destinations.

This website

The WhiteFel website does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or contact forms. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact WhiteFel at felderkwek@gmail.com.
