Common questions

How do I start a montage?

Tap Import from Photos and select your photos and videos in the order you want to use them. Once the montage opens, tap a clip in the timeline to edit it.

How do I trim, split, or rearrange clips?

Open a clip’s Edit Video screen and choose Trim & Split. Adjust the range handles to choose the section you want. Move the playhead inside the selected range to split it. To change the montage order, hold a thumbnail in the timeline, drag it beside another clip, and use the before/after indicator to choose where to drop it.

Where are the speed and reverse controls?

Open Edit Video and select Speed & Reverse. Use the whole-clip speed control to change the overall pace, or move the playhead and choose Set Speed Point at Playhead. Changing the whole-clip speed clears existing speed points.

Toggle Reverse Video to reverse the action in the live edited preview. Reversing mutes that clip’s original audio. The first reverse preview can take longer to prepare.

How do Pan & Zoom and keyframes work?

Choose Pan & Zoom from a clip’s editing controls. Drag the preview to pan and pinch to zoom, or use the sliders. Choose a time and add or update a keyframe to save the view. Add keys at different times to create motion between views.

If the image no longer fills the frame, recenter it or increase the zoom to cover the empty area.

Where are my saved montages?

Open the montage gallery from the home screen to reopen saved projects. Project media is stored inside the app. Deleting a saved project removes the app’s project copy; it does not delete the originals from Photos.

How do I export and share my movie?

Use the montage’s export control to create an MP4. Open the export preview and choose Share MP4, then select the destination or app in the system share sheet. Exporting can take longer for larger projects, reverse playback, and reframed footage.

An import or preview is taking a long time. What can I try?

If the selected media is stored in iCloud Photos, allow Photos to finish downloading it. Keep the app open while it prepares a reverse preview or export, and check that your device has enough free storage. If a particular clip fails, try importing it again or using a shorter range.

How much will Little Reel cost?

Little Reel is planned as a paid app with a one-time purchase and no advertising. The price has not been announced. A download link will be added when the app is available.
